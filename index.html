03Hypervelocity Engineering

Engineered with HVE Core

We deliver with Hypervelocity Engineering (HVE), using HVE Core, Microsoft’s open-source agentic SDLC framework for context management, governance, and shift-left engineering. Every pilot runs through the same disciplined loop, so speed never costs you traceability. AI carries the rules, humans keep the judgment.

  1. rpi-research

    Research

    Agents gather evidence from your data, systems, and constraints before any decision is made.

  2. rpi-plan

    Plan

    Findings become an explicit, reviewable plan that your planners approve before work begins.

  3. rpi-implement

    Implement

    Agents execute the approved plan step by step, guided by coding standards and guardrails.

  4. rpi-review

    Review

    Results are checked against the plan, with durable evidence and handoff artifacts at every step.
